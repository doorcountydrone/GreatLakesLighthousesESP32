Great Lakes Lighthouses

Open this page on the chart (setup: 192.168.4.1, or the chart LAN IP).

List order is LED order (south to north). LED 1 is first on the strip. Skip a light to leave that LED off. Tap an LED number to light only that LED for a few seconds — works on GreatLakes-Setup. Save when the strip matches.

Home Wi-Fi

This is the router the chart joins for internet — not GreatLakes-Setup. Leave password blank to keep the one already saved.

Keep flashing leaves every light on its real characteristic. Populate one by one goes dark, then lights each lighthouse in list order with the name on OLED or matrix.

Used when Light show is Populate one by one. 180 is 3 minutes of all lights flashing. 10 is 10 seconds per light on OLED or matrix.

Lighthouse strip is always on (D8 / GPIO 8). OLED uses D4 and D5 (GPIO 6 SDA, GPIO 7 SCL) and the board 3.3 V pin. Matrix data is D3 / GPIO 5, powered from 5 V. The light sensor uses D6 (held at 3.3 V) and D2 (input). OLED and matrix can run together.

IP once at startup. Weather only lists lights with rain/fog/snow/lightning. If none, GREAT LAKES LIGHTHOUSES every 15 seconds.

1 is slow, 10 is fast. 7 matches the original speed. Text enters from the right.

How many times each message scrolls across the OLED or matrix. 1 is once, then it waits until the text changes.

Max is the bright-room ceiling (1 to 30). Min is the dark-room floor (0 = off, 1–2 is a faint glow).

Sleep schedule

Sleep times are local. US daylight saving starts the second Sunday in March and ends the first Sunday in November. The chart sets its clock after it joins Wi-Fi.
