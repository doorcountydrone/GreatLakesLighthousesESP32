Great Lakes Lighthouses — Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32C3
=====================================================

Power
-----
- XIAO ESP32C3: USB-C.
- WS2812 / NeoPixel strip and the 8x32 matrix: 5 V sized for the LED count.
  13 strip LEDs is easy from USB. A long strip plus the matrix needs its own
  5 V supply.
- Common GND between the XIAO, the strip, and the matrix is required.
- SSD1306 OLED: 3.3 V and GND from the XIAO pins. Do not power it from a GPIO.
- Data pins are 3.3 V. Do not connect a 5 V data line back into the XIAO.

Pins (silk label on the board)
------------------------------
  Function              Pad    GPIO
  Strip data            D8     8
  LED matrix data       D3     5     8x32 WS2812, 5 V + common GND
  OLED SDA              D4     6
  OLED SCL              D5     7
  Setup button          D1     3     momentary button to GND; hold ~3 s at boot
  LDR 3.3 V             D6     21    held high; powers the light sensor only
  LDR input             D2     4     analog input

  Leave these alone:
    D9 / GPIO 9 is a strap pin. Holding it low at reset puts the chip in
    download mode.
    GPIO 12–17 are the internal flash bus.
    GPIO 18 and 19 are USB.

  D8 / GPIO 8 is the strip data pin and is also a strap pin. The data line
  should sit low while the board resets. Do not pull D8 high at power-up.

  Light sensor: D6 (3.3 V, always on) -- LDR -- D2 -- 10k resistor -- GND.
  D6 can only supply the sensor. The OLED still uses the board 3.3 V pin.
  D0 and D7 are free.

  OLED: copy ssd1306.py next to main.py. writer.py and sans18.py are optional;
  without them the screen uses the built-in 8x8 font. sans20.py is the larger
  second line when writer.py and sans18.py are also present.
  LED matrix: copy led_matrix.py next to main.py. Snake-column wiring. If
  letters look mirrored, change WIRING in led_matrix.py to COLUMN_MAJOR,
  SNAKE_ROW, or ROW_MAJOR.

  The strip, OLED, and matrix all run at once. Extra display "OLED and LED
  matrix" is the default.

LED order (south to north)
-------------------------
  0  Kewaunee Pierhead
  1  Algoma Pierhead
  2  Sturgeon Bay Ship Canal Light
  3  Sturgeon Bay North Pierhead
  4  Sherwood Point
  5  Eagle Bluff
  6  Chambers Island
  7  Baileys Harbor Range Lights
  8  Old Baileys Harbor (Birdcage)
  9  Cana Island
 10  Plum Island Range Lights
 11  Pilot Island
 12  Pottawatomie (Rock Island)

If your physical strip is longer, extra LEDs stay off. Set num_leds in setup
to the number of WS2812 chips on the wire.

Colors and flash (real characteristics)
---------------------------------------
  White  W   Canal-style lantern white
  Red    R   navigation red (pierhead, canal, ranges)

  Fl     flashing (short on, longer off)
  Iso    isophase (equal on and off)
  F      fixed (steady)
  Fl(2)  group of two flashes in one period

See README.md for each LED's official characteristic.
